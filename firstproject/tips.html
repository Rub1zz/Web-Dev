Easy Tips for Better Photos

  • Rule of thirds: put your subject off-center, not in the middle.
  • Golden hour: shoot right after sunrise or before sunset for soft light.
  • Get closer: fill the frame with your subject.
  • Watch the background: make sure nothing distracting is behind your subject.
  • Hold steady: keep your elbows in or use a tripod.

Camera Settings

Aperture

Shutter Speed

Controls motion blur.

ISO
