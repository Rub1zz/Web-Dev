Beginner Gear

You do not need expensive equipment to start. Here is what most beginners use.

ItemWhat It DoesPrice Range
SmartphoneGood camera you already own$0
Entry-level DSLR or mirrorlessMore control over settings$400 - $800
Kit lens (18-55mm)Works for most everyday shotsUsually included
TripodKeeps the camera steady$25 - $100
Memory cardStores your photos$10 - $30

What to Buy First

  1. Start with your phone and learn the basics.
  2. Get a cheap tripod for night and landscape shots.
  3. Upgrade to a real camera once you know what you like to shoot.
